Every hop here is a deliberate decision: Front Door terminates TLS at the edge and absorbs the WAF cost before traffic touches compute. The cache sits beside the app tier because a 94% hit ratio is the cheapest database you'll ever buy. Writes that don't need to block the user go through the queue — backpressure becomes a metric instead of an outage. And the workers scale to zero, because idle compute is just money on fire with extra steps.
